Honest residuals in LensJudge — We replaced LensJudge's lens-light "residual" view (the image that removes the foreground galaxy so lensed arcs pop, and which the AI grader sees on every candidate). The old version was a Gaussian high-pass that quietly lied to the data: it threw away the sign (hiding over-subtraction), left a 4-lobe "butterfly" artifact on elliptical galaxies that mimics arcs, and had no noise scale. The new version subtracts a proper elliptical-galaxy model and shows a signed, noise-normalized chi = (data - model)/sigma on a fixed +/-5 sigma red/blue scale, per band — so red excess is a candidate arc, blue is over-subtraction, and a real lensed arc has to be coherent across g and r. On real DR11 cutouts it cleanly reveals arcs in known lenses (e.g. the Cosmic Horseshoe ring) while refusing to fabricate one for lrg+companion mimics, and it passes synthetic honesty tests (residuals track N(0,1), injected arcs survive, over-subtraction stays visible). It's deployed everywhere the residual is used, and there's a short write-up with side-by-side examples and references here: https://usfcs-edu.github.io/agentic-lensing/current/lensjudge-residual/
