\subtitle{DESI-resolution cutout panels for the three panel-qualified NEW v3 grade-A candidates of Table~11}

\section{The candidates}
This atlas shows the imaging for the three \emph{new} strong-lens candidates that
\cnet{}~v3 surfaced in the DR10 vetting and that survived the campaign
qualification panel (advocate/\allowbreak skeptic/\allowbreak morphology/\allowbreak contaminant, skeptic veto,
2-of-$N$ A-rule) --- i.e.\ Table~11 of the \cnet{} report (\texttt{papers/main.pdf}).
The object ids and grades are read from \texttt{v3/cv3\_new\_gradeA.csv}; the
sky positions are joined from \texttt{v3/manifests\_d2\_newA.csv} (whose ranking
score equals the table's C-vet \plens{}). Each cutout is a grz \texttt{ls-dr10}
image at the finder's native geometry ($101$\,px, $0.262\arcsec$/px), fetched and
rendered with the group's inspection-viewer renderer (Lupton $Q{=}8$, vertical
flip, $400$\,px upsample), so these are exactly the pixels the v3 model and the
panel saw. For each object we show three panels: the \emph{full} $101$-px cutout,
a $2.5\times$ center \emph{zoom}, and a lens-light-subtracted \emph{residual} that
exposes the low-surface-brightness arc/ring hiding under the lens-galaxy light.

\paragraph{Honest framing.} Table~11 as first produced listed \emph{four}
candidates; we exclude \texttt{s\_310364\_6649}, which a crossmatch against the
Huang+2020 DECaLS catalogue identified as the already-published grade-A lens
\texttt{DESI-038.2078-03.3906} ($0.2''$ match) --- a recovery, not a new
candidate. The remaining three positions have \emph{no} Euclid coverage, so
they are DESI-resolution-\emph{qualified candidates, not confirmations}. At the
DECaLS $\sim$$1.3\arcsec$ seeing the ring-vs-partial-arc distinction is
genuinely uncertain at $0.262\arcsec$/px; higher-resolution imaging (Euclid/HSC,
HST, or Keck AO) is required to confirm them and measure an Einstein radius.
