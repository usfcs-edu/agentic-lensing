Panel (f) of this composite (row 2, right; 3.5" zoom) is a SIGNED-CHI residual shown as two
tiles, SW | LW (the short- and long-wavelength NIRCam channels, left to right): chi = (data -
smooth elliptical galaxy model) / noise, where the model follows the galaxy's own ellipticity
and position angle, is centred on the catalogued galaxy, and the result is displayed on a fixed
+/-5 sigma red/blue scale. Red/blue is the SIGN of data-minus-model, NOT the source's colour.
Judge by SHAPE and two-band coherence, never by sign: a real lensed arc is a TANGENTIAL /
curved feature OFFSET from the galaxy centre and present in BOTH tiles -- it may appear red OR
blue here, and a genuine lensed source can show either, so do NOT treat blue as "artifact".
The nucleus is imperfectly modelled, so the inner ~0.5" shows a central red/blue dipole, and
on a bright or elongated deflector saturated red/blue lobes aligned with its major and minor
axes can extend to ~1": IGNORE that inner region and any lobe pattern that is symmetric about
the centre, they are known model artefacts and evidence neither for nor against a lens.
Genuine artefacts to discount are a thin ring EXACTLY concentric with the core and any feature
present in only one tile. When one channel is missing the panel is a single tile and two-band
coherence cannot be tested; judge by shape alone.
